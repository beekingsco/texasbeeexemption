## Key Takeaways for BeeKings

1. **Mann Lake is the price leader** for basic unassembled hive kits at $149.99 for a complete hive
2. **Hoover Hives offers the best value** for wax-dipped equipment — their deep body kit at $59 is hard to beat
3. **Betterbee is premium-positioned** — higher prices but strong reputation and assembled options
4. **Bulk discounts are modest** — Hoover Hives maxes out at 10% for $5K+ orders; Mann Lake requires direct contact
5. **Free shipping thresholds**: Mann Lake $150+, Hoover Hives $50+ (best), Betterbee varies
6. **All three ship nationwide** — Mann Lake also has 4 physical stores

### Opportunity for BeeKings:
- Mann Lake's $149.99 complete unassembled kit is the benchmark competitors must beat
- Hoover Hives' wax-dipped finish at $129 (1D+1M) offers a unique selling point
- There's a gap in the market for a **Texas-local** supplier of unassembled kits with competitive pricing
- Mann Lake's dealer program could be worth exploring for resale margins
