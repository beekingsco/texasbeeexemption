## Notes for BeeKings Calculator Integration

1. **Most popular pickup corridor:** I-35 from San Antonio through Austin, Waco, and DFW
2. **East TX is underserved** — Ames Farm is the only major nuc producer east of I-45. Club networks (NETBA in Canton, Kaufman Beekeepers, Rusk County) are critical for connecting buyers with local small-scale producers.
3. **Bulk orders:** Lockhart Honey Farms is the clear winner for operations needing 5+ nucs
4. **BeeWeaver in Navasota** is the most historically significant Texas operation (since 1888) — their treatment-free genetics are highly regarded
5. **Average 2026 nuc price in Texas:** ~$240 for a quality 5-frame nuc with marked queen
