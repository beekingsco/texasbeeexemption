## TROUBLESHOOTING

### If Main Numbers Don't Work:
1. Try state's 311 information line
2. Google "[State] property tax division contact"
3. Try extension service main number - they often know who to call
4. Try county assessor in state capital county - they'll know state contacts

### If You Get Voicemail:
Leave message: "Hi, I'm researching agricultural property tax exemptions for beekeeping and have a few quick questions about [STATE]'s program. Please call me at [NUMBER] or email [EMAIL]. Thank you!"

### If Transferred Multiple Times:
- Keep track of names and extensions
- When you reach right person: "Thank you! Before we start, what's your direct number in case we get disconnected?"
