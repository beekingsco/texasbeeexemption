# Louisiana Agricultural Use Value Assessment - History Requirements

## Question: Does Louisiana require a history of agricultural use before qualifying?

**ANSWER: It depends on acreage.**

## Key Findings

### Two Pathways to Qualify (RS 47:2303)

| Acreage | History Requirement |
|---------|-------------------|
| **3+ acres** | NO history required - can qualify in Year 1 |
| **Under 3 acres** | Must show $2,000 average gross annual income for **4 preceding years** |

### Statutory Requirements

**RS 47:2303 - Eligibility for use value assessment:**
> In order to be classified as bona fide agricultural, horticultural, marsh or timber land and assessed at its use value...it must meet the definition of bona fide agricultural, horticultural, marsh or timber land as described in R.S. 47:2302 and, in the case of bona fide agricultural, horticultural, or timber land:
>
> A. Be at least **three acres in size**, or have produced an average gross annual income of at least **two thousand dollars** in one or more of the designated classifications **for the four preceding years**, and
>
> B. The landowner has signed an agreement that the land will be devoted to one or more of the designated uses

### Definition of Bona Fide Agricultural Land (RS 47:2302)

> "Bona fide agricultural land is land devoted to the production for sale, in reasonable commercial quantities, of plants and animals, or their products, useful to man..."

### Application Process (RS 47:2304)

- Must file application with parish assessor
- Application is **permanent** as long as property remains eligible (except Orleans and Jefferson Parishes - must refile every 4 years)
- Must sign agreement that land will be devoted to designated agricultural uses
- If property is sold, new owner must sign new application within 60 days

## Summary

**Louisiana is MORE favorable than Texas for new agricultural operations:**

- **3+ acres**: Qualify immediately in Year 1 with no income history required
- **Under 3 acres**: Need 4 years of income history ($2,000/year average)

Unlike Texas (which requires 5-7 years of history depending on county), Louisiana allows immediate qualification for properties 3 acres or larger.

## Constitutional Basis

Louisiana Constitution Article VII, Section 18(C):
> "Bona fide agricultural, horticultural, marsh, and timberlands, as defined by general law, shall be assessed for tax purposes at ten percent of use value rather than fair market value."
