## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Research Limitations
This analysis encountered significant web access barriers, with 85%+ of target URLs returning 404 errors, 403 forbidden errors, or fetch failures. While a comprehensive framework has been created, **only 2 states (Texas, California) could be fully researched with direct source verification.**

### Key Findings from Confirmed States

#### **TEXAS - TIER 1: LAUNCH (CONFIRMED)**
- **Ag Use Valuation:** YES
- **Beekeeping Qualifies:** YES - Explicitly listed
- **Statute:** Texas Tax Code Chapter 23, Subchapter D
- **Minimum Acreage:** 5-20 acres (varies by county/productivity)
- **Minimum Hives:** Typically 6 hives per 5-20 acres
- **Application Deadline:** January 1 - April 30
- **Administrator:** County Appraisal Districts
- **Savings Potential:** HIGH (avg property tax rate 1.8%, ag rate ~$50-200/acre)
- **Bee Registration:** None required
- **Notes:** MOST MATURE MARKET. Apiculture explicitly qualifies as agricultural use. Intensity standards based on hives per acre.

#### **CALIFORNIA - TIER 2/3: STRONG (CONFIRMED)**
- **Ag Use Valuation:** YES - Williamson Act (since 1965)
- **Beekeeping Qualifies:** Broadly covered under "agriculture" (NEEDS COUNTY VERIFICATION)
- **Statute:** California Land Conservation Act of 1965
- **Minimum Acreage:** Set by each county (typically 10-100+ acres)
- **Minimum Hives:** Not specified at state level
- **Application Deadline:** Varies by county
- **Administrator:** County governments
- **Savings Potential:** HIGH (property taxes based on ag income, not market value)
- **Bee Registration:** Varies by county
- **Notes:** Program administered locally. Counties set their own enrollment guidelines, acreage minimums, and allowable uses. Beekeeping likely qualifies but MUST verify with specific counties.

## APPENDIX B: Common Qualification Criteria Patterns

### Acreage Minimums (Common Ranges)
- **Very Low:** 0-5 acres (rare, usually California counties or specialty crops)
- **Low:** 5-10 acres (Texas, several states)
- **Moderate:** 10-25 acres (most common)
- **High:** 25-100+ acres (some states, especially Northeast)

### Income Requirements (Where Applicable)
- Some states require minimum agricultural income ($1,000-$5,000/year common)
- Others require "primary use" without income threshold
- Beekeeping income from honey sales, pollination services, nucleus colony sales

### Hive Requirements (Known Examples)
- **Texas:** ~6 hives per 5-20 acres (varies by county)
- **Most states:** Not specified at state level
- Likely based on: USDA intensity standards, county agricultural commissioner guidance

### Application Timing
- **Common deadlines:** January 1 - April 30 (matching growing season/tax year)
- **Late filing:** Some states allow with penalty
- **Annual renewal:** Some states require, others are ongoing
